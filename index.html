About

Data that answers the question

I deliver data-driven solutions that people actually use — analysing complex datasets, building interactive dashboards, streamlining reporting and surfacing the insights behind strategic decisions. Most of my work sits at the meeting point of business intelligence, predictive analytics and the ETL that feeds both.

At Elevance Health I build Power BI dashboards on advanced DAX to track healthcare KPIs, tune SQL and Python that cut report execution time by 35%, and run ETL on BigQuery, Databricks and Spark. Before that I built Tableau reporting for client engagements at Accenture, and started out at Wells Fargo modelling enterprise data warehouses and Azure Data Factory pipelines for regulatory reporting.

A lot of my value comes from removing manual work — report automation at Elevance saved roughly 15 hours a week — and from setting data governance and quality standards that make stakeholders trust the numbers in front of them.
